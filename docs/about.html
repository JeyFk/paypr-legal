About Paypr and our guides

By Paypr · Updated September 8, 2026

Paypr is an iPhone app by Vladyslav Danin, published under the QAtion brand. It helps you record hourly work, payments and outstanding balances. The App Store listing identifies the developer and current app details.

Two sides of hourly work

Track people you pay, such as a nanny, babysitter, tutor or cleaner. In contractor view, track clients who pay you, clock your work and share a statement of unpaid hours. Each view keeps its own records; it is not a shared employer-and-worker account.

How we prepare the guides

Paypr publishes these resources to help readers plan costs and keep better records. Product explanations are based on the app. Numerical examples identify their assumed rates and hours. External rate figures should identify their source and reference year, with the relevant limitations.

BLS childcare-worker wage data does not include private household workers. Marketplace booking averages describe that marketplace’s bookings. Neither is a universal rate recommendation. Our babysitter rate guide separates these sources.

Scope of the information

Our guides are written by the Paypr publisher, not presented as a review by a tax or legal professional. For applicable requirements, consult the official sources linked in each guide. Paypr does not calculate or file taxes or transfer money.
