Nanny tax calculator: 2026 employer FICA estimate

Estimate the employer's Social Security and Medicare contribution for one household employee. This is one part of employment cost, not a complete tax calculation or a take-home-pay calculator.

US federal rules · Tax year 2026 · Sources checked September 18, 2026 · By Paypr

Use this example only for a household employee age 18 or older who is not your spouse, child, or parent. Family relationships, age, and other exceptions need a separate check in IRS Publication 926. This tool does not determine whether someone is an employee.

Include wages paid throughout the calendar year, including any overtime and taxable bonus wages. For future planning, enter your annual cash-wage estimate. Start with the wage calculator if you need a simple hours-based estimate. Cash wages include payments by check and similar methods; this does not mean only banknotes.

2026 federal calculation

Under the assumptions above, household FICA applies when cash wages to this employee reach $3,000 for the year. Once that threshold is reached, the calculation uses all covered cash wages, not just the amount above $3,000.

  • Employer Social Security: 6.2% of covered wages, up to the 2026 wage base of $184,500.
  • Employer Medicare: 1.45% of covered wages, without a wage-base cap.

Example: $49,920 in annual covered wages produces $3,095.04 of employer Social Security and $723.84 of employer Medicare: $3,818.88 in employer FICA. This is not the total employer tax bill.

Costs and obligations this does not calculate

Federal unemployment tax (FUTA) has a separate quarterly test across household employees. State unemployment and other state obligations can also apply. This estimator does not calculate FUTA, state taxes, employee withholding, income taxes, insurance, benefits, or service fees.

It also excludes the cost of paying the employee's FICA share yourself. Additional Medicare withholding at higher wages has no employer match. A result of $0 here does not mean there are no filing, wage, or tax obligations.
