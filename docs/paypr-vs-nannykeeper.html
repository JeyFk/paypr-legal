Paypr vs NannyKeeper: hours tracking or payroll tools?

By Paypr · Updated September 8, 2026

Choose Paypr when you want a personal iPhone record of hourly work, payments and balances. Consider NannyKeeper when you want a web workflow with nanny time entry and household payroll tools. This comparison is written by Paypr, so check both products against your own needs.

What each product does

TaskPayprNannyKeeper
Daily recordsHours, hourly rates, paid and unpaid entries, partial paymentsWeb time tracking and payment records
How the nanny logs timeSeparate personal records in contractor view; no shared employer portalLink-based entry connected to the employer dashboard
Tax toolsNo tax calculation, filing or money transferPaid tools for tax calculations and year-end documents
Other hourly workSeparate household and client viewsFocused on household employment
Free optionOne person per view; Pro adds summaries and exportFree tracking tier; payroll features require a paid plan

NannyKeeper feature statements checked September 8, 2026 against its product page and time-tracker page. Verify current plans and exactly who submits filings before subscribing. Neither product is affiliated with the other.

Try the workflow you will use every week

In Paypr, add a person and rate, record a shift, then record a partial payment. Check whether the remaining balance is the answer you need. If the person whose hours you track must enter time directly into your records, Paypr’s two personal views do not provide that shared workflow.

Can an hours tracker handle payroll obligations?

Paypr provides records. Any required withholding, tax payments and filings need to be handled separately, either by you or with appropriate help. See the hours-tracker and payroll guide.
