Questions, answered

Is there a private way to track the hours I work for multiple clients as an independent contractor?

Yes. Paypr's contractor view keeps a separate rate, hours log and unpaid balance for every client you work for, and the records never leave your control: they're stored on your iPhone and sync only through your own private iCloud account. There's no company account, no shared workspace and no employer dashboard — clients see nothing unless you choose to share a statement with them, and no client can see that any other client exists. Analytics never include client names, rates, hours or amounts, and nothing is sold or used for ads. Track one client free forever.

How do I track the hours I work for each client?

Add each client once with your hourly rate for them, then clock in when you start working — or log hours afterwards on a timeline. Paypr keeps a separate running tally per client of hours worked and what's still unpaid.

Do my clients need the app?

No. Your records live in your own app and iCloud. When it's time to get paid, share a clean statement — a simple summary of hours worked and the total unpaid — by message, email or print.

Can I use Paypr for both sides — people I pay and people who pay me?

Yes. Paypr has two views: one for tracking people you pay by the hour, and a contractor view for tracking clients who pay you. Switch anytime in Settings — each side keeps its own separate records, and switching never mixes them.

Is Paypr free for contractors?

You can track one client free forever. Paypr Pro unlocks unlimited clients, weekly/monthly/yearly summaries and export — start with a 7-day free trial, then $2.99/month or $7.99 once for lifetime.

How do I keep records for taxes as an hourly independent contractor?

Log the date, hours and rate for each session as you work, and record every payment you receive with its date and amount, so each client has an accurate paid and unpaid history. That gives you the income record you need for a Schedule C and quarterly estimated taxes, and evidence if a client disputes an invoice. Paypr Pro exports weekly, monthly and yearly summaries as CSV so you can hand the year straight to an accountant.
